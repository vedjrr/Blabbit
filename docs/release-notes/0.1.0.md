First public release of Blabbit: private, offline dictation for Apple Silicon Macs.

- Hold a shortcut, speak, release: text appears at your cursor in any app.
- Runs entirely on your Mac. Choose from Parakeet, Whisper, SenseVoice and Moonshine models, with measured accuracy and speed scores.
- Exact, Clean, Code, Professional and Custom modes; personal vocabulary; searchable history that stays on your Mac.

**First open:** Blabbit isn't notarised by Apple yet. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. Later updates install from inside the app.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon.
